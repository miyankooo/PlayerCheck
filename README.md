# PlayerCheck

Check any player's gear, logs and rankings from one right-click, without typing their name.

- **Right-click** a player
- **Pick** Armory Links
- **Copy** their Blizzard armory, Raider.IO, Warcraft Logs, Armory WoW Forever or aotc.gg link

## Why use it

- **In every player menu.** Portraits, chat names, friends, Battle.net friends, guild and community rosters and more.
- **One keypress per link.** Tab moves to the next link, and a check mark shows each one you copied.
- **Nothing to set up.** Links match your region and ruleset on their own.

## Features

- One window lists every link, each in its own box under the site's name
- Close After Copying closes the window after a copy. Turn it off to copy several links in a row
- Each link always holds the whole address, so a stray keypress can't break it
- Raider.IO and Warcraft Logs links use your ruleset, Normal, PvP, RP or RP-PvP, which every player in your world shares
- Works in every region Forever runs in, Americas, Europe, Korea and Taiwan, with names in any alphabet

## Quick start

1. Right-click a player portrait, chat name or roster row
2. Pick **Armory Links**
3. Click a link, or press Tab to reach it, then press Cmd+C on macOS or Ctrl+C on Windows
4. Paste the link into your browser

## Commands

`/pc intro` and `/pc help`

## Good to know

- The Blizzard armory lists characters from level 10
- Warcraft Logs lists only characters seen in an uploaded log, and aotc.gg only characters it has scanned
- Armory WoW Forever covers the Americas and Europe
- A Battle.net friend's menu shows only the armory and aotc.gg, since the friend list doesn't say which ruleset they play on
