# Player Armory Link

A WoW Forever addon that adds Armory Links to every player right-click menu. It shows the player's pages on the Blizzard armory, Raider.IO, Warcraft Logs, Armory WoW Forever and aotc.gg, ready to copy, so you can see their gear, logs and rankings in your browser without typing their name and surname.

## Features

- Armory Links in every player menu: portraits, chat names, friends, Battle.net friends, guild and community rosters and more.
- One window lists every link, each in its own box with the site's name above it. Tab moves to the next link, and a check mark shows each link you copied.
- One keypress copies the selected link. Close After Copying closes the window after a copy; turn it off to copy several links in a row.
- Each link always holds the whole address, so a stray keypress can't break it.
- Raider.IO and Warcraft Logs links use your ruleset, Normal, PvP, RP or RP-PvP, which every player in your world shares.
- Works in every region Forever runs in: Americas, Europe, Korea and Taiwan, with names in any alphabet.
- Nothing to set up.

## Usage

1. Right-click a player portrait, chat name or roster row.
2. Pick Armory Links.
3. Click a link, or press Tab to reach it, then press Cmd+C on macOS or Ctrl+C on Windows.
4. Paste the link into your browser.

Commands: `/pal intro` and `/pal help`.

## Good to know

- The Blizzard armory lists characters from level 10.
- Warcraft Logs lists only characters seen in an uploaded log, and aotc.gg only characters it has scanned.
- Armory WoW Forever covers the Americas and Europe.
- A Battle.net friend's menu shows only the armory and aotc.gg, since the friend list doesn't say which ruleset they play on.
